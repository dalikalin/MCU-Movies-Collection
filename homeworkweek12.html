MCU MOVIES COLLECTION

Hello there!

Glad to have you here. Are you looking to explore something new? Welcome to the Marvel Cinematic Universe which is a massive, interconnected saga where genius inventors, cosmic gods, and neighborhood heroes share the same world. Every film delivers its own standalone thrill while building toward epic, universe spanning crossover events. Whether you're here for the laugh out loud comedy, mind blowing action, or incredible character journeys, consider this your ultimate, zero stress guide to jump in and start watching!

Marvel structures its films into thematic eras:

The Infinity Saga
- Phase 1 (2008-2012): Iron Man, The Incredible Hulk, Iron Man 2, Thor, Captain America: The First Avenger, The Avengers.
- Phase 2 (2013-2015): Iron Man 3, Thor: The Dark World, Captain America: The Winter Soldier, Guardians of the Galaxy, Avengers: Age of Ultron, Ant-Man.
- Phase 3 (2016-2019): Captain America: Civil War, Doctor Strange, Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2, Spider-Man: Homecoming, Thor: Ragnarok, Black Panther, Avengers: Infinity War, Ant-Man and the Wasp, Captain Marvel, Avengers: Endgame, Spider-Man: Far From Home.
The Multiverse Saga
- Phase 4 (2021-2022): Black Widow, Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, Eternals, Spider-Man: No Way Home, Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, Thor: Love and Thunder, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever.
- Phase 5 (2023-2025): Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania, Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3, The Marvels, Deadpool & Wolverine, Captain America: Brave New World, Thunderbolts.
- Phase 6 (2025+): The Fantastic Four: First Steps, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, Avengers: Doomsday, Avengers: Secret Wars.
